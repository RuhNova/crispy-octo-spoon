Planetary Highlights

Mercury

Closest to the Sun, with extreme temperature swings and no thick atmosphere.

Earth

Our ocean world with active plate tectonics and a protective magnetic field.

Mars

A cold desert planet with giant volcanoes and traces of ancient water systems.

Jupiter

The largest planet, a gas giant with powerful storms like the Great Red Spot.

Saturn

Known for iconic rings made of ice and rock particles orbiting in thin bands.

Neptune

Deep blue giant with supersonic winds and methane-rich upper atmosphere.

Core Physics Concepts

Gravity

F = G(m₁m₂ / r²)

Massive objects attract each other; distance weakens the force quickly.

Orbital Motion

v = √(GM / r)

Planets stay in orbit when forward velocity balances gravitational pull.

Relativity

E = mc²

Energy and mass are equivalent, linking matter to cosmic-scale dynamics.
